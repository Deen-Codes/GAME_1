# Bay City

A portrait, free-roam 3D city game for iPhone, built with three.js in plain HTML/CSS/JS (no build step). It runs straight from GitHub Pages.

## The world
- **Downtown:** a large grid city with towers, shops, parks, parking lots and traffic.
- **Bay Beach & pier:** along the whole south coast, with the ocean beyond.
- **Hidden Hills:** rolling hills to the west under the **BAYWOOD** sign, with mansions for sale. The map ends in cliffs.
- **Eastport:** a seaside suburb to the east, linked by highway and the coast road.
- **Northfield & Bay City Airport:** to the north, with a runway, terminals, a control tower, hangars and planes.
- **Day/night cycle:** a 12-minute day. Windows and street lamps light up at night, and your car has working headlights.

## Controls
Hold your phone upright. The game opens straight into the city wherever you left off.

| Button | On foot | Driving |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom | Context action: Talk / Get in / Jack car / Break in / Play | Exit (or Race / Sell car) |
| Right | Jump | Gas |
| Left | Fire / Punch / Swing / Throw | Brake / reverse |
| Top | Reload | Horn |

- **Weapon button** (top left): tap to holster or unholster. Hold and slide for the **weapon wheel**: fists, bat, pistol, SMG, shotgun, assault rifle, sniper, RPG and grenades, with ammo counts.
- **Radio** (in the car): tap for the next station. Hold and slide for the **radio wheel**.
- **Phone**, usable on foot or in a stopped car: Maps (waypoints and GPS), Messages (customer orders and tips), Contacts (relationships), Me (needs and skills), Places, Wallet, Garage (all your cars), Homes, Style, Radio and Settings.
- **Tap the minimap** to jump straight into Maps.
- **Conversations** play out like a Pokemon battle screen: they face you, you see the back of your head, subtitles type out, and you pick what to say.

Keyboard: WASD move/steer, Space jump, J fire, R reload/horn, E action, 1-9 weapons, H holster, P phone, M map.

## Life sim
- **Needs:** hunger, energy and mood (the three bars under the clock) drain over time. Eat at diners, bars and QuickMart, or buy groceries and eat from your own fridge. Sleep in your bed. Mood rises with TV, music, dancing, good food and winning.
- **Skills** level up from 1 to 10 by doing things:
  - Strength (gym, heavy bag) gives harder hits.
  - Stamina (treadmill, running, deliveries) lets you run faster.
  - Health raises max HP.
  - Charm (reading, chatting, DJing, dancing) unlocks better dialogue, haggling and faster friendships.
  - Driving makes cars faster and grippier.
  - Dealing gets better prices and bigger orders.
  - Every gun has its own skill, trained at the Bay Arms shooting range. It means tighter aim and faster reloads.
- **Relationships:** every dealer, shopkeeper, bartender and trainer remembers you, from Stranger to Known, Friendly, Trusted and Family. Friends give discounts, tips about hot prices and better haggles.

## Living city
- Animated ocean with shallow turquoise water, rolling waves and foam on the shore. Fountains and pools ripple.
- Every window looks into a real room with depth (walls, floors, furniture, people, curtains) that shifts as you move. About half the rooms light up at night.
- Shops, venues and homes have proper front doors with lit signs. The marker sits at the door.
- Pigeons, crows and seagulls peck around and fly up to lamp posts when you get close. There are also stray cats, people walking their dogs, and squirrels. Deer graze in the hills by day. At night raccoons, possums, coyotes and owls come out.
- Homeless people sleep rough on the pavements. Talk to them, give money or food, and they may share tips or a hidden stash.
- Beachgoers wear swimwear, which you can also buy at Venice Surf Co.
- Bay Chapel: sit and reflect, talk to Father Gabe, donate, and use the food bank once he trusts you.

## Romance
- Flirt with women around the city. Compliments are safe, cheesy lines and bold moves are risky, and beach flirts work best at the beach. Charm, mood and night-time all help.
- Get her number, or ask her to hang out. She follows you around and the heart button opens your date chat: kiss, talk, buy her a drink, invite her back to yours.
- WooHoo Sims-style, under the duvet with a bouncing bed, or in a parked car with steamy windows. It fades to black.
- Women you've dated are in Contacts. Ask them out again from your phone.

## Family
- Make it official, propose (a $2,500 ring), and book a $5,000 wedding at Bay Chapel with Father Gabe. Your wife moves into your family home.
- WooHoo with "try for a baby" and she may fall pregnant. Her belly grows over 90 days. When her water breaks, choose a home birth or rush her to Bay General.
- Kids grow from baby to toddler, child, teen and adult. Cuddle, play, help with homework, teach them to drive, and give pocket money. Teens text you for cash.
- Neglect your family and they'll let you know. Cheat and your partner may find out.
- Messages has real chats with your partner, dates and kids. Text them, ask them out and keep the relationship alive.

## Homes
- Each home can be your family home, rented out for daily income, left vacant, or sold for 75% of its price. You can't sell the home your family lives in.

## Hospital
- Bay General Hospital: reception, doctor, ward beds, check-ups and pregnancy scans. You wake up here after blacking out or collapsing from hunger.

## Drinking and getting high (all fictional goods)
- Beer, shots, whiskey and cocktails at the Anchor and the Neon Room. Get tipsy and the screen blurs, you stagger and the car drifts. Drink too much and you black out.
- Use what you buy from dealers (phone > Wallet > Use):
  - Blue Haze: slow, saturated colours, munchies.
  - Red Pills: speed and punch power, red pulse, then a crash.
  - Pixie Dust: rainbow world with sparkles.
  - Moon Rocks: low gravity and huge jumps.
  - Glowstix: neon inverted city.
  - Gold Leaf: golden glow and +3 Charm.
- Mixing too much means an overdose and a trip to hospital.

## Police
- Crimes give you wanted stars: punching, shooting, jacking, running people over, robbing, explosions. Patrol cars in traffic make it worse.
- Cop cars chase you with sirens. Officers jump out, try to arrest you (1-2 stars) or open fire (3+ stars).
- Lose them by staying out of sight, hide at home, or pay the fine at Bay City PD.
- Busted: fined, stash confiscated, and you wake up in a cell at the station.

## Fighting
- Tap Punch for jab, jab, hook, uppercut combos (the bat swings, backhands and goes overhead). Kick is on the Y button.
- People take several hits. Some fight back, others run.
- The Pit (Eastport) is an underground cage-fight ladder of 5 fighters. Jab, hook, block their wind-ups, counter, and bet on yourself with Sal the bookie.

## Streets
- Traffic lights at every downtown junction, and traffic stops on red.
- Brake and reverse lights on every car.
- Every person has their own name. Dog walkers bring their dog into conversations, and the conversation backdrop matches where you are (city, suburb, beach, pier, hills, airport).
- Bay Waste binman job: drive the bin lorry round 8 bins, paid per bin plus a bonus.

## Cars, jacking and crashes
- Climb in and out of cars with an animation. Exit at speed to bail out and roll, while the car keeps going.
- Jacking drags the driver out. Brave drivers get up and fight you or chase their car, and can pull you back out. The rest run off.
- Cars collide with each other. Traffic stops for parked cars and turns around if blocked.
- 9 rim designs and 6 tyre types, all visibly different. Upgrades cost money at Bay Customs; the phone's Garage sets GPS there.
- Weapon and radio wheels: tap to open, tap to pick.
- The Work app lists every way to make money.

- Car controls: bottom = Drive, left = Brake/Reverse, right = Shoot (drive-by, auto-aims), top = Get out. No speedo.
- Real damage: panels dent where you hit, paint darkens and headlights break. Smoke turns from grey to black, then the car catches fire and blows up.
- Fuel stations and the Suds car wash repair your car for a fee.
- Bicycles, rental e-scooters ($2) and motorbikes are parked around town.

## Getting around on foot
- Swim in the sea and fountains. Some pedestrians swim too.
- Jump at a wall to grab the ledge and climb up. Fire-escape ladders lead to rooftops. Dive off into water; long falls on land hurt.

## Emergency services
- Explosions start fires. Fire engines come to put them out, and ambulances come to collect the injured.

## Police (reworked)
- A crime someone calls in puts a red search circle on the minimap. Get out of it and stay unseen for about 3 seconds and you're clear. Hiding inside the circle works too, just slowly.
- If a cop sees you, the chase is on. Every cop and police car has a vision cone, always shown on the minimap. Buildings block their view, and it's shorter at night.
- Stars climb one at a time. Five stars needs a real rampage. Reinforcements come from behind you.
- After you escape, units patrol the area (blue dashed circle). If a patrol spots you, the chase is back on.
- Drug deals in view of a cop get called in. Passers-by sometimes snitch.
- A fuel station, car wash or Bay Customs clears your stars once the cops have lost sight of you.

- When an officer catches you on foot, you get a choice:
  - At 1-2 stars: talk your way out. Your first 1-star offence gets a warning.
  - At 1 star: pay a fine on the spot.
  - At up to 3 stars: offer a bribe.
  - Any time: go quietly for half the fine and sentence, or shove past and run.
- Cops only shoot if you're armed, at 3+ stars in a car, or at 5 stars. If they take you down, you get arrested, not hospitalised.

## Camera
- Pinch the game view (or use the mouse wheel) to zoom right in and see faces and clothes, or zoom out. Double-click to reset.

## Prison: Bay County Correctional
- Busted at 2+ stars means prison (12h to 3 days), in an orange jumpsuit. Your phone and weapons are taken. Free roam: cell block, canteen (3 free meals a day), yard (weights, hoops), visiting room, infirmary.
- Lights out at 22:00 fast-forwards to morning. Your bunk lets you nap or do your time.
- Inmates: Big Tony (shot caller: shivs, work, riots), Dre and Moose (fights), Pops (lifer, knows a way out), Rico (talks too much), Lil Mikey (new fish). Guards Grimes and Pike. Mama Joy runs the canteen. Agent Walsh (FBI) offers a deal.
- Ways out: serve the time, post bail, bribe, community service (an unpaid bin round), a lawyer or bail bondsman on the pay phone, Pops' spoon tunnel, or a riot.
- Escaping gives you 4 stars. Change clothes at home or a clothes store.
- Starving in prison sends you to the prison infirmary, not out the door.

## Farms
- Barns, silos and hay. Tractors plough the fields (hop in and drive one, or buy one). Cows, horses, sheep, pigs, goats and chickens.

## Driving extras
- Indicators: traffic signals before it turns. Your car signals the next GPS turn.
- Hold Brake + Drive for wheelspin. Let go of the brake to launch. Steer while holding both for donuts.
- Tow trucks haul away burnt-out wrecks. Emergency services and police follow the roads.
- Mood rises when you're well fed, rested and healthy.

## Homes and furniture
You start with a free studio downtown. Bigger apartments, houses and the Baywood mansion can be bought. Buy furniture at Casa Bay; it's delivered to your storage. Inside any home, tap the pencil button to edit: pick up, move, rotate, store or add furniture. Furniture is usable:
- bed: sleep
- fridge and stove: eat and cook
- TV: watch
- bookshelf: read for Charm
- desk: laptop market prices
- weights and treadmill: train
- stereo: music
- aquarium: relax
- arcade: play
- safe: extra stash space

## Making money
- **Dealers:** buy low, sell high. Prices change daily and by area.
- **Customers** text you orders. Bring the goods to them before they give up.
- **Jobs:** Bay Cabs taxi shifts and courier deliveries.
- **Casino:** blackjack and slots.
- **Real estate:** pays daily income.
- **Car crime:** jack or break into cars and sell them at Lucky's Chop Shop.
- **Activities:** street races, Taco Rush, surf school, Muscle Beach, hoops and DJ sets.

## Cars
Buy new cars at Bay Motors. There are 16 bodies from a $6k hatchback to the $160k hypercar, including the new SUV, convertible, wagon, lowrider, classic, box truck and hypercar. All your cars live in the phone's Garage, and every one can be customised (bumpers, spoilers, rims, suspension, engine, paint, decals, tint, underglow and more).

Your progress saves automatically on this device. Each player has their own save.
